# Research Paper Report: An Information Integration Theory of Consciousness

**Author:** Giulio Tononi
**Publication Year:** 2004
**Venue:** *BMC Neuroscience*, 5:42
**DOI/arXiv:** `10.1186/1471-2202-5-42`
**Category:** (4) Integrated Information Theory (IIT)
**Role in Our Work:** **Adjacent theory, directly relevant to an open question, not a threat to novelty.** Answers `theory/open_questions.md` Q3 by giving the precise object ($\Phi$) our theory needs to be related to.

---

## 📌 Abstract & Architecture
Original statement of Integrated Information Theory (IIT). Proposes that consciousness corresponds to a system's capacity to integrate information — formalized as $\Phi$, the amount of information generated by a system as a whole, above and beyond the information generated independently by its parts, when the system is partitioned along its "weakest link" (minimum information partition). $\Phi$ is intrinsic and observer-independent, computed from a system's own causal structure (its repertoire of cause-effect states), not from external behavior.

## 🔗 Connection to Computational Coupling Theory
Directly addresses the open question already recorded in `theory/open_questions.md`: *"What is the exact relation between $C_{\text{couple}}$ (inter-system) and Tononi's Integrated Information $\Phi$ (intra-system)?"* The key structural distinction to make explicit in the theory paper: $\Phi$ measures information integration *within* a system's own causal boundary (intrinsic, closed under partition), whereas Coupling Capacity $C(i\to j;B)$ measures directed information transfer *across* a boundary between two systems through a bandwidth-constrained interface. One natural conjecture worth stating formally: high-$\Phi$ systems (highly integrated internally) should be the systems best able to convert Transfer Entropy into task-relevant capacity per bit — i.e., $\Phi$ may be a structural precondition for high performance on Prediction 2 (better self-predictive world models extract more capacity per bit), since a system that poorly integrates its own state is also a poor self-predictor. This paper is the primary citation for that conjecture; it should NOT be cited as evidence against our novelty, since IIT explicitly excludes inter-system, interface-mediated coupling from its scope.
