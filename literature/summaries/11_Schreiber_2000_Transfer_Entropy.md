# Research Paper Report: Measuring Information Transfer

**Author:** Thomas Schreiber  
**Publication Year:** 2000  
**Venue:** *Physical Review Letters*, 85(2):461  

---

## 📌 Core Formulations
Introduced Transfer Entropy ($\mathrm{TE}_{X 	o Y}$):
$$\mathrm{TE}_{X 	o Y} = I(Y_{t+\Delta} ; X_{\le t} \mid Y_{\le t})$$
Conditions out $Y$'s past to prove directional, model-free causal information transfer between coupled dynamical time series.
