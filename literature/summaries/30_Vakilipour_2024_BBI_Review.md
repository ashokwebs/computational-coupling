# Research Paper Report: Brain-to-Brain Interface Technology: A Brief History, Current State, and Future Goals

**Authors:** Pouya Vakilipour, Saba Fekrvand
**Publication Year:** 2024
**Venue:** *International Journal of Developmental Neuroscience*, 84(5):351–367
**DOI/arXiv:** `10.1002/jdn.10334`
**Category:** (7) Recent (2023-2026) work explicitly on brain-to-brain interfaces — adjacent/competing-novelty watch item
**Role in Our Work:** **Adjacent survey, low novelty threat but should be read carefully.** It is a historical/engineering review, not a competing theory of coupling capacity.

---

## 📌 Abstract & Architecture
A 2024 narrative review of brain-to-brain interface (BBI) technology: traces the field from Pais-Vieira (2013, canon #1) and Rao (2014, canon #2) through BrainNet (2019, canon #3) and later systems, covering the standard BBI architecture (a brain-computer-interface "read" stage feeding a computer-brain-interface "write" stage, mediated by a digital/network link) and surveying invasive vs. non-invasive read/write modalities, current technical limitations, and near-term future directions.

## 🔗 Connection to Computational Coupling Theory
This is the paper most likely to be cited by a reviewer asking "hasn't someone already reviewed this space?" — the honest answer is yes, but as a *history-and-engineering* survey, not as a measurement theory: it catalogs BBI hardware/software architectures and milestones but does not propose any information-theoretic capacity construct, does not define a bandwidth-vs-effective-dimension saturation law, and does not address multi-agent/MARL coupling at all. It is useful primarily as (a) a single up-to-date citation covering the historical lineage already in our canon (Pais-Vieira, Rao, BrainNet) so we don't have to individually re-justify field maturity, and (b) a "future goals" section worth checking against our own roadmap to confirm no other group has already proposed a Coupling-Capacity-like construct — as of this review, none has. Worth a periodic re-check (every 6-12 months) given how fast this specific niche is moving in 2024-2026.
