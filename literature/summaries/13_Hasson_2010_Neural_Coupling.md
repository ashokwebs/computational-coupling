# Research Paper Report: Speaker-listener neural coupling underlies successful communication

**Authors:** Greg J. Stephens, Lauren J. Silbert, Uri Hasson  
**Publication Year:** 2010  
**Venue:** *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS)*, 107(32):14425  

---

## 📌 Summary
fMRI hyperscanning showing listener brain state tracks speaker brain state with a 1-3 second temporal delay during successful natural speech communication.
