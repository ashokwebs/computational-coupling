## 📌 Core Formulations
Established Channel Capacity $C = \sup_{p(x)} I(X;Y)$. Bedrock of information theory. We generalize Shannon capacity from static discrete channels to dynamic coupled state trajectories ($C_{	ext{couple}}$).
