## 📌 Abstract & Theoretical Framework
Formalizes interpersonal biobehavioral synchrony, communication, and therapeutic alliance within Karl Friston's **Active Inference / Free Energy Principle** framework. Communicating agents are modeled as coupled statistical engines that update their generative models to minimize mutual prediction errors (variational free energy).

## 🛠️ Mathematical Formulation
Free energy $F$ of the coupled system is bounded by:
$$F = D_{	ext{KL}}[q(s) \parallel p(s)] - \mathbb{E}_{q(s)}[\log p(o \mid s)]$$
Communication emerges as mutual prediction-error minimization, driving the two agents into a shared hermeneutic niche.

## 📊 Key Insights
