# Research Paper Report: Hyperscanning: Simultaneous fMRI during linked social interactions

**Authors:** P. Read Montague et al.  
**Publication Year:** 2002  
**Venue:** *NeuroImage*, 16:1150  

---

## 📌 Summary
Pioneered hyperscanning by linking two fMRI scanners over the internet during social interaction games. Historical baseline for multi-subject neural recordings.
