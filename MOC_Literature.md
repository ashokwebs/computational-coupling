# 📚 Map of Content: Literature Canon & Paper Summaries

> [!abstract] **Overview**
> Comprehensive breakdown of all 15 core papers underpinning neural decoding, brain-to-brain interfaces, optimal transport alignment, emergent AI communication, and active inference.

---

## 🗂️ Categorized Paper Vault

### 1. Brain-to-Brain Interfaces (Hardware & Triggers)
- 🐭 **[[literature/summaries/01_Pais_Vieira_2013_Rat_BBI|Pais-Vieira et al. (2013)]]** — Rat motor cortex ICMS microstimulation (~70% accuracy, 1-bit trigger).
- 👤 **[[literature/summaries/02_Rao_2014_Human_BBI|Rao & Stocco et al. (2014)]]** — First human non-invasive BBI via EEG motor imagery to TMS phosphenes.
- 🧩 **[[literature/summaries/04_Jiang_2019_BrainNet|BrainNet (Jiang et al. 2019)]]** — 3-person BBI Tetris network with signal reliability trust weighting.

### 2. Emergent AI Communication & Active Inference
- 🤖 **[[literature/summaries/03_Foerster_2016_DIAL_MARL|DIAL (Foerster et al. 2016)]]** — Emergent communication protocols via backprop through noisy channels.
- 🧘 **[[literature/summaries/06_McParlin_2022_Active_Inference|McParlin & Friston (2022)]]** — Interpersonal alignment as mutual Variational Free Energy minimization.

### 3. Optimal Transport & Representation Alignment
- 🌐 **[[literature/summaries/05_Thual_2022_FUGW_Optimal_Transport|FUGW (Thual et al. 2022)]]** — Fused Unbalanced Gromov-Wasserstein alignment for heterogeneous brains.
- 📊 **[[literature/summaries/14_Chen_2015_Shared_Response_Model|SRM (Chen et al. 2015)]]** — Shared Response Model for multi-subject fMRI factorization.
- 🚨 **[[literature/summaries/10_Nakamura_2024_Representation_Transfer|Nakamura et al. (2024)]]** — Unsupervised hyperspherical transfer (**Primary Novelty Threat**).

### 4. Brain Foundation Models & Decoders
- ⚡ **[[literature/summaries/07_Jiang_2024_LaBraM_EEG_Foundation|LaBraM (Jiang et al. 2024)]]** — EEG foundation model pre-trained on 2,500 hours via VQ-NSP.
- 🧠 **[[literature/summaries/08_Caro_2024_BrainLM_fMRI_Foundation|BrainLM (Caro et al. 2024)]]** — Generative fMRI foundation model trained on 6,700 hours across 424 parcels.
- 🖼️ **[[literature/summaries/09_Scotti_2024_MindEye2|MindEye2 (Scotti et al. 2024)]]** — Shared-subject visual perception reconstruction mapped to unCLIP space.

### 5. Information Theory & Hyperscanning Foundations
- 📐 **[[literature/summaries/12_Shannon_1948_Information_Theory|Shannon (1948)]]** — Channel Capacity $C = \max I(X;Y)$.
- 🔀 **[[literature/summaries/11_Schreiber_2000_Transfer_Entropy|Schreiber (2000)]]** — Transfer Entropy ($\mathrm{TE}_{X \to Y}$).
- 🗣️ **[[literature/summaries/13_Hasson_2010_Neural_Coupling|Hasson et al. (2010)]]** — Speaker-listener neural coupling fMRI hyperscanning.
- 💻 **[[literature/summaries/15_Montague_2002_Hyperscanning|Montague et al. (2002)]]** — First dual fMRI scanner hyperscanning.
