### Is Brain-to-Brain Communication Science or a Sci-Fi Circus?

Bro, I couldn't sleep at all last night. 

I was pacing around my room at 3 AM asking myself the exact same thing over and over: **Why tf does almost every BBI paper feel like a cheap party trick?**

Look at the literature:
- Miguel Nicolelis' rat-to-rat motor paper (2013): Encoder rat presses lever, motor signal gets zapped into decoder rat via ICMS, decoder rat presses lever.
- Rao & Stocco (2014) human BBI: Person A thinks about moving hand, EEG picks up motor imagery, zaps Person B's motor cortex via TMS to click a button.

Okay, hardware engineering is cool, sure. But scientifically? 
**Bro... THAT IS NOT COMMUNICATION!** 

That's just building a glorified remote-control button with 1 bit of throughput. It's binary trigger zapping! Where is teh actual meaning? Where is the information exchange?

Every time people talk about BBI or "Brain Languages" or "Brain Communication Protocols" (BCP), they jump straight to trying to design a protocol. They say: *"Oh, let's map voxel cluster X to word Y!"* or *"Let me build a neural decoder that outputs English tokens!"*

Wait... hold on. Isn't that fundamentally broken?

### Random questions burning in my head this morning:
1. **What is a "Brain Language" even supposed to mean?** Does the brain even have a discrete dictionary built in? No! Brain states are continuous, non-stationary dynamical trajectories moving on high-dimensional manifolds!
2. **Why are we designing protocols before defining channel capacity?**
3. When Claude Shannon was trying to connect radios in 1948, he didn't start by picking an arbitrary dictionary of words. He asked: *What is the maximum theoretical rate of error-free information transfer over a noisy medium?* He defined Channel Capacity $C = \max I(X; Y)$.
4. What is the equivalent of Shannon's Channel Capacity when sender and receiver aren't static discrete message generators, but **dynamic, self-predictive intelligent systems** whose internal state vectors are constantly evolving?

If we dont answer Question 4 first, any BCP we design is just an arbitrary engineering hack — like trying to design 56k modems before Shannon defined bits!

We need a real, rigorous, substrate-independent measurement theory. Not sci-fi telepathy hype, but something that can stand tall at NeurIPS, ICML, Nature Neuroscience.

Time to grab another coffee and scribble out some math.
