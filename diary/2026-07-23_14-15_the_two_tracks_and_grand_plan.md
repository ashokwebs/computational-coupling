### The Grand Masterplan: 2 Tracks, 4 Papers 🗺️

Over lunch today, I sat down with a napkin and sketched out the roadmap for the entire research program.

Bro, we have to be super disciplined here. There's a trap every theoretical ML/neuro researcher falls into: **endless theory polishing without building empirical evidence.** I am NOT gonna let that happen to us!

### Detailed Breakdown of the Roadmap:

#### Track 1 — Finishing the Foundational Paper (`paper/`)
- **Introduction & Motivation:** Write the killer hook contrasting fixed protocol engineering vs general measurement theory.
- **Related Work:** Integrate citations scraped yesterday (Hasson 2010, Nicolelis 2015, Kraskov 2004, Schreiber 2000, Tononi IIT 4.0).
- **Falsifiability / Scope Section:** Add exact mathematical conditions under which the theory fails.
- **Discussion & Limitations:** Computational complexity of transfer entropy estimators, non-stationarity of neural manifolds, ethical considerations of stimulation.

#### Track 2 — The Multi-Year Empirical Roadmap
This is where we turn theory into undeniable empirical proof across 4 sequential papers:
1. **Paper 1 (NeurIPS / ICML)** — *Multi-Agent RL Validation* (`PettingZoo` cooperative games, sweeping channel bandwidth $B \in [1..32]$ bits/step).
2. **Paper 2 (Nature Neuroscience / ICLR)** — *Biological Validation via Hyperscanning* (OpenNeuro `ds007764` DUET dataset and `ds007471` Joint Agency EEG).
3. **Paper 3 (Nature Machine Intelligence)** — *Causal Manipulation Paradigm* (Manipulating feedback latency and bandwidth non-invasively).
4. **Paper 4 / Prototype (Nature / NeurIPS)** — *The Learned Brain Communication Protocol (BCP)* (Using $C_{\text{couple}}$ as an explicit loss function to learn a closed-loop BBI prototype!).
