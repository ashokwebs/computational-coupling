## 🎯 The 4 Papers Breakdown

```
Paper 1 (NeurIPS / ICML) ──► Paper 2 (Nature Neuro / ICLR) ──► Paper 3 (Nature Mach Intell) ──► Paper 4 (Nature Prototype)
MARL Bandwidth Sweep         Biological EEG Hyperscanning      Causal Feedback Intervention      Learned Closed-Loop BCP
```

### 1. 🚀 [[experiments/paper1_rl/README|Paper 1: Multi-Agent RL Validation]]
* **Target Venues:** NeurIPS 2027 / ICML 2027
* **Core Objective:** Validate Predictions 1 & 2 in `PettingZoo` cooperative games (`simple_speaker_listener_v4`), sweeping quantization bandwidth $B \in [1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32]$ bits/step.

### 2. 🧠 [[experiments/paper2_hyperscanning/README|Paper 2: Biological Validation via Hyperscanning]]
* **Target Venues:** Nature Neuroscience / ICLR 2028
* **Core Objective:** Test Predictions 1--3 against public human dyadic EEG corpora (OpenNeuro `ds007764` DUET, `ds007471` Joint Agency EEG) using FUGW functional alignment and LaBraM latent embeddings.

### 3. ⚡ Paper 3: Causal Manipulation Paradigm
* **Target Venues:** Nature Machine Intelligence 2028
* **Core Objective:** Non-invasive causal intervention manipulating feedback latency and bandwidth in human dyads.

### 4. 🏆 Paper 4: The Learned Brain Communication Protocol (BCP)
* **Target Venues:** Nature / NeurIPS 2029
* **Core Objective:** Use $C_{\text{couple}}$ as an explicit differentiable loss function to train a closed-loop BBI prototype.
