## Headline result

Coupling capacity saturates at a ceiling set by the **smaller system's effective
representational dimensionality**, not by the channel's raw bit-rate (measured slope
$\bar{c}\approx0.39$ bits/dimension). Engineering implication for BBI/BCI: past a critical
bandwidth, a wider channel buys nothing — raise the receiver's usable dimensionality instead.
